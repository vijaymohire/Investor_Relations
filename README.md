# Investor Relations

## Overview

This repository contains the **investor-facing content, demonstrations, presentations, facts, evidence, and supporting materials** used to communicate the Bhadale IT / QAI opportunity to prospective investors, strategic partners, organizations, and other stakeholders.

It is focused on presenting the business, technology, products, capabilities, demonstrations, development progress, and investment-related information in a structured manner.

For the broader startup, entrepreneurship, business-development, training, procurement, proposal, and commercialization work, see the **[Entrepreneurship repository](https://github.com/vijaymohire/entrepreneurship)**.

---

## Purpose

The repository provides a consolidated location for materials used in:

- Investor demonstrations and presentations
- Startup and company capability presentations
- Technology and product demonstrations
- Business and market discussions
- Strategic partnership discussions
- Funding and investment discussions
- Corporate / MNC discussions
- Technology and innovation showcases

The objective is to make relevant investor-facing information easier to organize, review, update, and present.

## Demonstration and Evidence

Investor-facing demonstrations should preferably connect business or technology claims with demonstrable evidence.

A useful communication sequence is:

**Business Opportunity → Technology / Product → Prototype / Demonstration → Evidence / Results → Commercialization Opportunity**

This helps distinguish between:

- Concept
- Research
- Prototype
- Demonstration
- Working implementation
- Product
- Commercial opportunity

and helps ensure that research concepts or early prototypes are not presented as production products.

## Relationship to Technical Repositories

Investor Relations is not intended to replace the technical repositories.

Technical implementation, source code, research, experiments, frameworks, and engineering assets should remain in their appropriate repositories.

The investor-facing repository can provide links to relevant technical evidence when deeper technical review is appropriate.

This creates a simple separation:

**Business / Entrepreneurship → Investor Relations → Selected Technical References → Technical Repositories**

The Investor Relations repository primarily supports executive, investor, business, and strategic-partner communication, while technical repositories provide deeper implementation and research evidence.

## Content Governance

Investor-facing material should be kept accurate and appropriately qualified.

Where applicable, distinguish between:

- Current capability
- Prototype
- Proof of concept
- Research
- Planned capability
- Future roadmap
- Commercial offering
- Demonstrated result
- Target or assumption

Future plans, research concepts, and target values should not be presented as established facts.

Sensitive information should not be placed in a public repository unless it has been intentionally approved for public disclosure.
